# Where Sales and Success Overlap

Leadership asked you to make the case for merging two roles, and the strongest version of that
case is showing exactly where the two teams' day-to-day work already overlaps — that overlap is
the entire argument, not a side note.

Sales owns work that Customer Success never touches: prospecting new accounts, negotiating
contract terms, and carrying the quarterly quota. Customer Success owns work Sales never touches
either: onboarding new customers, running the renewal process, and handling escalated support
issues. But sit in on either team's week and you'll see both of them independently doing three
things that neither role technically owns: checking in on account health, having upsell
conversations with existing customers, and acting as the customer's main point of contact for the
relationship. Right now two different people from two different teams are doing those same three
things for the same account, often without knowing the other one is doing it too.

That duplication — both sides already doing the same three things independently — is exactly the
case for combining the roles. Make sure the slide shows what's unique to Sales, what's unique to
Success, and that these three specific activities sit in both at once, because the overlap itself
is what leadership needs to see to approve the merge.
