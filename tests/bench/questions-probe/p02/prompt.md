# Customer Feedback Loop Walkthrough

Support and product are aligning on the four-stage routine they just adopted for turning customer
complaints into shipped fixes, and you're building the slide that explains the routine to the
wider team at next week's town hall.

The four stages: first, Capture — every complaint from the in-app widget and support tickets gets
logged in one place, no exceptions. Second, Triage — the team reviews the backlog every Monday,
tags each item, and ranks it by how many customers hit the same issue. Third, Build — engineering
picks up the top-ranked items and ships a fix or a small feature. Fourth, Verify — someone goes
back to the specific customers who originally complained and confirms the fix actually solved
their problem for them, not just in theory.

The point everyone needs to walk away with: this never stops. The moment Verify wraps up on one
batch, the team turns straight back around and starts Capturing the next batch — there's no
finish line, no "project complete," it's meant to keep circling for as long as the product exists.
Make that continuous, always-returns-to-the-start nature of the routine unmistakable on the
slide, not just implied by stage order.

Tone: practical, internal working-team language, no marketing gloss.
