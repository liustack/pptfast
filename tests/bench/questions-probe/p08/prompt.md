# Product Demo Slide for the App Launch Deck

You're building the slide in the launch deck where the audience gets their first look at the
actual product. Marketing was firm about one thing: this can't read as "a picture on a slide" —
the moment someone glances at it, they need to immediately register "that's a phone in someone's
hand" before they even process what's on the screen itself. A screenshot floating in a plain
rectangle reads as a stock photo; the same screenshot presented so it unmistakably reads as a
phone reads as a real product.

Use the attached screenshot of the dashboard screen — it's the actual home screen a user sees
right after opening the app, showing today's summary numbers and the three quick-action buttons.
Keep the rest of the slide restrained: a short headline above it introducing the screen, and one
or two callouts pointing at specific things worth noticing (the summary numbers redesign, the new
quick-action row), but the screen itself has to stay legible and dominant — it's the actual
product, not decoration around a headline.
