# Product Screenshot for the Series A Deck

You're finishing the Series A deck for a logistics-optimization SaaS company, and this is the
slide where investors get their first look at the actual product. The founder was specific about
one thing: this cannot read as "a screenshot pasted on a slide." The moment someone glances at
it, they need to register "that's our software, running live in a browser" before they process
what the dashboard is actually showing — a bare rectangle of pixels reads as a mockup someone
drew in Figma, the same screenshot presented so it unmistakably reads as software running in a
real browser window reads as a real, working product that a real customer is really running
today.

Use the attached screenshot of the route-optimization dashboard — it's the actual screen a
dispatcher sees mid-shift, showing today's active routes and the live reassignment queue. Keep
the rest of the slide restrained: a one-line headline above it, and a single callout pointing at
the reassignment queue (the thing that actually saves customers money), but the screen itself has
to stay legible and dominant — investors are paying for thirty seconds on this slide, most of it
should land on the product, not on decoration around it.
