# New Engineering Org Announcement

Engineering just finished a reshuffle and you're writing the all-hands slide that explains the
new reporting lines before the rumor mill fills the gap. People need to see exactly who now
reports to whom, three levels deep, not just a list of names and titles floating with no
connection between them.

At the top, Elena Vance (CTO) now has three VPs reporting directly to her: Raj Patel (VP
Platform), Sofia Kim (VP Product Engineering), and Grace Liu (VP Applied AI). Below that layer,
each VP has their own directors reporting to them, and the split is uneven on purpose — Raj has
two directors under him (Mia Chen running Infra, Tom Alvarez running Data), Sofia has three (Ben
Osei on Mobile, Priya Nair on Web, Leo Fischer on Growth), and Grace has just one for now (Diego
Marin on ML Platform, with more hires planned once the team scales). Make sure the slide actually
shows which director sits under which VP — that grouping is the whole point of the announcement,
people are specifically asking "wait, who do I report to now" and a name badge without a
connecting line back to their VP doesn't answer that.

Keep it factual and calm — this is an internal clarity slide, not a celebration slide. Audience is
the full engineering org, many of whom are anxious about the change.
