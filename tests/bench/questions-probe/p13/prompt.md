# Sponsor Thank-You Slide for the Conference Recap Deck

You're closing out the post-conference recap deck with the sponsor thank-you slide — the page
that goes out to every attendee and every sponsor after the event wraps. All eight sponsors
backed the event at the same tier this year, so the slide's entire job is to let all eight marks
land at a glance, evenly, with nothing implying one sponsor mattered more than another — no
ranking, no size differences, no supporting text under any of them, just the marks themselves
doing the work.

Use the eight sponsor logo files in the attached assets — treat every one of them as equal weight
and keep each mark fully legible and unclipped; a sponsor logo losing its own wordmark to a crop
is exactly the kind of mistake that gets noticed and complained about. Nothing else competes for
attention on this slide besides a short "Thank you to this year's sponsors" line above the marks.
