# Partner Ecosystem Slide for the Series B Deck

You're finishing the Series B deck and need the slide that proves market validation through
adoption rather than through another paragraph of claims. Investors skim this kind of slide in
about three seconds, so the job is to let twelve real company names land at a glance, evenly, with
nothing else competing for attention — no ranking, no logos singled out as bigger or more
important, no supporting sentences under any of them. The message is entirely "these twelve
companies already integrated us into their stack," nothing more needs to be said about any single
one of them.

The twelve companies currently live on the platform: Northwind Freight, Cobalt Analytics, Harbor
Robotics, Vantage Health, Ridgeline Foods, Meridian Capital, Bluepeak Energy, Solace Retail, Arden
Biotech, Frontline Logistics, Copper Cloud, and Lumen Materials. Treat all twelve as equal weight —
this is a wall of proof, not a leaderboard, and pulling any one of them out for special treatment
would undercut the "this is just how many people already trust us" point the slide exists to make.

Audience is growth-stage VCs who've seen a hundred of these decks and can tell in half a second
when a company padded the list — keep it exactly the twelve real names, nothing invented to fill
space.
